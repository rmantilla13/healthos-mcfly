healthOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

healthOS is a private health dashboard at health.mcfly.bot, run by Ricky Mantilla. It is not open to the public: accounts are created only from an invite link sent by an admin. Each person who joins gets their own private dashboard.

What data it uses

  • Your account: your email address, the name you enter, and your password, which is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • WHOOP, read through WHOOP's official API only after you sign in to WHOOP and approve access. healthOS reads recovery, sleep, physiological cycles and strain, workouts, basic profile details (first name) and body measurements. It never writes to WHOOP.
  • Apple Health, from the healthOS iPhone app (which sends daily summaries) or a Health export you import.
  • An optional profile photo.
  • Ask McFly, the optional AI assistant: the questions you ask it. Your conversation is kept only in your browser tab, and healthOS stores just a daily count of your questions and how much processing they used.

How it is used

Your data is used only to show you your own charts, trends and summaries. healthOS has no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no profiling. It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

Ask McFly

When you ask McFly a question, healthOS sends your question, the conversation so far, and the parts of your health data McFly reads to answer it to Anthropic's API, which generates the reply. McFly can only read your own data, and it can't change anything. Anthropic handles this data under its commercial terms and privacy policy. If you don't use Ask McFly, nothing is sent to Anthropic.

Who can see it

  • Only you can see your health data. Other members can't see it.
  • Admins manage accounts. They can see your email, name, role, when you last signed in, and the health of your syncs: how many days of data you have and its date range, when Apple Health and WHOOP last synced, whether a sync failed, and how many questions you asked McFly. The admin screens don't show your health values or your questions.
  • Admins can disable or delete your account and create a password-reset link for you. They never see or set your password.
  • The operator runs the database and so has technical access to it, but does not look at members' health data.

Where it is stored

  • The app is hosted on Vercel. Data is stored in a Postgres database run by Neon in the United States (US East).
  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Reading any data requires signing in. The only cookie is a sign-in session cookie, which is strictly necessary.
  • WHOOP access tokens and iPhone app tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to other people.

Service providers

These providers handle data only to run the service: WHOOP (a data source), Vercel (hosting), Neon (database) and Anthropic (answers for Ask McFly). Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your data is kept until you remove it or your account is deleted.
  • You can remove your Apple Health data from the Integrations page at any time.
  • Disconnecting WHOOP in healthOS revokes the app's access with WHOOP and deletes the stored tokens. You can also revoke access from WHOOP's own account settings.
  • To delete your account, ask the admin. Deleting an account removes everything stored for it: health data, settings, tokens and photo.
